Simple home-style appetizers

  • Garlic Bread or Cheesy Garlic Toast: French bread slices slathered with garlic butter and toasted until golden (add melted mozzarella for a cheesy variant)
  • Classic Deviled Eggs: Hard-boiled eggs halved and filled with a creamy mix of mayo, mustard, a pinch of paprika, and a dash of pickle relish.
  • Pan-Seared Mini Sausages (Cocktail Sausages): Small sausages quickly browned in a skillet and tossed in a sweet-and-savory honey mustard or BBQ glaze.
  • Toasted Tortilla Chips with Homemade Salsa: Store-bought tortillas cut into triangles, brushed with oil, and baked until crisp, served with fresh chopped tomato salsa.
  • Cucumber Cream Cheese Bites: Crinkle-cut cucumber slices topped with a dollop of seasoned cream cheese and a sprinkle of black pepper or dill.

Easy Home-Style Salads

  • Simple Tomato & Cucumber Salad: Diced tomatoes and cucumbers tossed with thinly sliced red onions, olive oil, salt, black pepper, and a splash of vinegar or lemon juice.
  • Classic Coleslaw: Shredded cabbage and carrots tossed with a quick, creamy dressing made from mayonnaise, a splash of milk, sugar, and vinegar.
  • Basic Green Garden Salad: A fuss-free mix of crisp iceberg or romaine lettuce, sliced cucumbers, and carrots, served with your favorite store-style or quick oil-and-vinegar dressing.
  • Easy Potato Salad: Boiled and diced potatoes tossed with mayo, hard-boiled egg bits, celery, mustard, salt, and pepper.
  • Sweet Corn and Pepper Salad: Canned or thawed sweet corn mixed with diced bell peppers, red onions, a drizzle of olive oil, lime juice, and a pinch of salt.
