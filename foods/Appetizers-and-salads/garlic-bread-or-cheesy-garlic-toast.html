Garlic Bread or Cheesy Garlic Toast Recipe and Preparation


Ingredients

  • 1 loaf of French bread or a baguette.
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened at room temperature.
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced or crushed
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried parsley).
  • A pinch of salt.
  • Optional: 1 cup shredded mozzarella or cheddar cheese.

Preparation

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice the French bread down the middle lengthwise or into individual diagonal slices.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the softened butter, minced garlic, parsley, and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  3. Spread the garlic butter evenly across the cut sides of the bread. If making cheesy toast, sprinkle the shredded cheese on top of the butter layer.
  4. Place the bread on a baking sheet and bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and toasted (broil for the last 1–2 minutes if you want extra crispiness). Slice and serve warm.
